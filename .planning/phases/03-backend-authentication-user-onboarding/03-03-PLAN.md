<threat_model>
### Security Threat Model (ASVS L1)
- **Threat 1**: Collecting sensitive personal health telemetry without explicit affirmative consent under India's DPDP Act 2023.
- **Threat 2**: Making unsupported medical or diagnostic claims during onboarding or wellness profiling.
- **Severity**: High
- **Mitigation**: Require explicit user consent checkbox before finishing onboarding; disclaim all Ayurveda/Dosha outputs as traditional lifestyle guidance and non-medical.
</threat_model>

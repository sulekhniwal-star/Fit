<threat_model>
### Security Threat Model (ASVS L1)
- **Threat 1**: Leaking sensitive health records, Indian phone numbers, or email addresses into production logs or crash reporting sinks.
- **Threat 2**: Hardcoding subscription prices or bypassing feature gates without authorization.
- **Severity**: High
- **Mitigation**: Built-in regex and field-name PII redaction filter in AppLogger. RemoteConfig enforces signed/authenticated server payloads with cached local defaults; zero hardcoded prices in code.
</threat_model>

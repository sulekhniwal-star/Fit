<threat_model>
### Security Threat Model (ASVS L1)
- **Threat 1**: Making unsupported diagnostic or clinical claims regarding elevated blood pressure or blood sugar.
- **Threat 2**: Medication schedule data leaks exposing ongoing treatments or health conditions.
- **Severity**: High
- **Mitigation**: Require statutory non-medical disclaimer on all vitals UI; encrypt medication schedules locally in SQLCipher; treat vitals as sensitive personal data under DPDP.
</threat_model>

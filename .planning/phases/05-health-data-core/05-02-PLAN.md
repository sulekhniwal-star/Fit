<objective>
Implement Daily Steps, Sleep, Water, and Mood Tracking with Bento Quick-Actions (TASK 027, 028, 029, 030).

Purpose: Enable comprehensive daily lifestyle logging completely offline, supporting overnight sleep analysis, quick-add water intake, and emotional wellness tracking.
Output: StepRecord, SleepRecord, WaterLog, MoodLog entities, Drift persistence tables, WaterQuickAddCard and MoodSelectorCard widgets, and test suites.
</objective>

<threat_model>
### Security Threat Model (ASVS L1)
- **Threat 1**: Unencrypted personal mood notes revealing mental health conditions exposed on device backups.
- **Threat 2**: Timezone shifts corrupting overnight sleep logs into two split fragmented sessions.
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Mitigation**: Store mood notes in encrypted Drift SQLite; compute sleep durations using UTC timestamps while attributing session to awakening date.
</threat_model>

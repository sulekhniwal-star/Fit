<objective>
Implement Core Health Primitives, Weight Logging & Body Measurements with Trend Analytics (TASK 025, 026, 032).

Purpose: Establish the standard biometric logging foundation enabling offline entry of weight and body dimensions, calculating 7-day moving averages, and automatically syncing via the offline outbox.
Output: Health primitives, BodyMetric domain and Drift table, WeightTrendCalculator, WeightLoggerSheet UI, and automated test suites.
</objective>

<threat_model>
### Security Threat Model (ASVS L1)
- **Threat 1**: Invalid or extreme measurement inputs causing overflow or skewed metabolic calorie targets.
- **Threat 2**: Data loss or duplicate metric submissions during offline logging.
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Mitigation**: Strictly validate metric boundaries (weight: 20-400kg, waist/hip: 30-250cm); enforce idempotent outbox synchronization.
</threat_model>

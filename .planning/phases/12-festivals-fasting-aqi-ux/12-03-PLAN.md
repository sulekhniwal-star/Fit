# Plan 12-03: Festival Calendar & Cultural Nutrition Planner

## Metadata
- **Phase**: 12 (Festivals, Fasting, AQI & India-First UX)
- **Plan**: 12-03
- **Focus**: Extensible Indian Festival Calendar, Festive Food Nutrient Profiles & Shame-Free Macro Balancing
- **Requirements**: `CULT-02`, `TASK 071`
- **Dependencies**: Phase 6 (Indian Nutrition Engine), Phase 9 (Daily Intelligence Package)

## Objective
Build an extensible, culturally authentic Indian festival calendar and nutrition planner. Deliver comprehensive traditional festive dish profiles (Laddoo, Gujiya, Modak, Biryani, Payasam, Kheer), optional user participation (never assume religion or cultural practice), and shame-free calorie balancing (weekly redistribution, fiber/hydration pre-loading, gentle recovery) integrated into the Daily Intelligence Package.

## Implementation Tasks

### 1. Festival Domain Models & Replaceable Calendar Abstraction
- Path: `lib/features/cultural/domain/festival_models.dart`, `festival_calendar_source.dart`
- Core Constructs:
  - `class FestivalEvent`:
    - `id` (String), `name` (String, e.g. "Diwali - Festival of Lights", "Eid ul-Fitr", "Holi", "Navratri"), `hindiName` (String, e.g. "दिवाली", "ईद-उल-फ़ितर"), `startDate` (DateTime), `endDate` (DateTime), `description` (String), `culturalGreeting` (String), `traditionalFoods` (List<FestiveFood>), `isFastingAssociated` (bool), `fastingProtocolType` (FastingProtocolType?).
  - `abstract class FestivalCalendarSource`:
    - `Future<List<FestivalEvent>> getUpcomingFestivals({DateTime? fromDate, int daysLookahead = 30})`
    - `Future<FestivalEvent?> getActiveFestival({DateTime? forDate})`
  - Extensibility Guarantee:
    - Default `StaticIndianFestivalCalendar` pre-loaded with major national and regional Indian festivals (Diwali, Holi, Eid ul-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Navratri/Dussehra, Makar Sankranti/Pongal, Ganesh Chaturthi, Onam, Raksha Bandhan, Christmas, Baisakhi).
    - Can be swapped for dynamic API or regional panchang without touching UI or business logic.

### 2. Cultural Nutrition & Traditional Festive Food Catalog
- Path: `lib/features/cultural/domain/festive_food.dart`, `festive_balance_calculator.dart`
- Festive Food Profiles:
  - Realistic portion sizes, authentic Indian recipe variations, and calibrated macros:
    - *Besan Laddoo*: 1 piece (45g) ~ 220 kcal, 4.2g protein, 26g carbs, 11g fat.
    - *Kaju Katli*: 1 diamond piece (20g) ~ 95 kcal, 2.1g protein, 11.5g carbs, 4.8g fat.
    - *Gujiya / Karanji*: 1 piece (60g) ~ 260 kcal, 4.5g protein, 32g carbs, 13g fat.
    - *Modak (Ukadiche)*: 1 piece (50g, steamed rice flour + jaggery/coconut) ~ 135 kcal, 2.0g protein, 24g carbs, 3.8g fat.
    - *Sheer Khurma*: 1 small katori (150ml) ~ 240 kcal, 6.5g protein, 34g carbs, 9g fat.
    - *Rice Kheer*: 1 katori (150ml) ~ 210 kcal, 5.2g protein, 31g carbs, 7.5g fat.
    - *Mutton/Chicken Biryani (Festive)*: 1 plate (300g) ~ 520 kcal, 26g protein, 58g carbs, 20g fat.
- `FestiveBalanceCalculator`:
  - Shame-free weekly balancing:
    - Suggests redistributing 150-250 kcal across the prior 3 days or subsequent 3 days rather than starving or fasting punitive measures.
    - Pre-meal hydration & fiber loading recommendations (2 glasses of water + cucumber/sprouts bowl 20 minutes prior to feast).
    - Post-feast walk suggestion (15-20 min light stroll to improve postprandial glucose regulation).

### 3. Festival Participation & Preferences State
- Path: `lib/features/cultural/presentation/festival_state_notifier.dart`
- Responsibilities:
  - Tracks user preferences: Users can mute or participate in specific festivals; never forced or inferred.
  - Generates contextual Daily Intelligence Package (DIP) cards when a celebrated festival is active or within 3 days.

### 4. Festival UI & Cultural Celebration Cards
- Path: `lib/features/cultural/presentation/festival_banner_card.dart`, `festival_detail_sheet.dart`, `festive_food_picker_sheet.dart`
- UI Elements:
  - Bento `FestivalBannerCard` on Dashboard with festive golden/saffron accents, warm cultural greeting in bilingual English/Hindi, and practical balance tips.
  - `FestivalDetailSheet` displaying the festival's significance, traditional healthy food options, and an interactive "Feast Planner".
  - Quick-add festive food picker seamlessly logging traditional sweets directly into the user's `DriftMealLogRepository`.

### 5. Automated Verification & Tests
- Path: `test/features/cultural/festival_calendar_test.dart`, `test/features/cultural/festive_nutrition_test.dart`, `test/features/cultural/festival_widget_test.dart`
- Test Scenarios:
  1. `FestivalCalendarSource` returns active and upcoming festivals for specified date ranges.
  2. Optional opt-in: Muting a festival removes its banner and DIP cards.
  3. Festive food macro accuracy matches ICMR / Indian food composition data.
  4. `FestiveBalanceCalculator` computes healthy, non-punitive calorie redistribution without zero-calorie extremes.
  5. Widget tests: `FestivalBannerCard` displays cultural greetings and festive tips cleanly.

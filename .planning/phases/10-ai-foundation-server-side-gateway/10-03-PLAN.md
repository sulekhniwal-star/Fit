# Phase 10 Plan 03: Photo Food Logging & User Confirmation UX

## Metadata
- **Phase**: 10 — AI Foundation & Server-Side Gateway
- **Plan**: 10-03
- **Focus**: Photo food logging, natural text meal logging modal, candidate confirmation card, and manual fallback (TASK 062, TASK 063)
- **Status**: Ready to execute

## Objective
Construct the user-facing AI logging surfaces in Flutter. Build the natural text meal analyzer sheet, the camera/gallery photo food logging interface, and the essential `AiMealConfirmationCard` that guarantees user verification, portion adjustments, and manual catalog fallbacks before committing AI-estimated meals to local encrypted Drift tables.

## Scope of Work

### 1. Presentation Components (`lib/features/ai/presentation/`)
- `ai_meal_text_logger_sheet.dart`:
  - Bottom sheet modal for freeform meal logging in English or Hinglish (e.g., *"2 butter roti, 1 katori dal tadka, aur thoda sa curd"*).
  - Quick meal preset chips (*"Ghar Ka Khana"*, *"Poha & Chai"*, *"Dal Chawal"*).
  - Analyze action with animated loading state.
- `photo_food_logger_screen.dart`:
  - Camera shutter / photo picker interface with image preview.
  - Explicit DPDP 2023 privacy banner: *"Photo is analyzed in real-time and immediately deleted. Zero permanent server storage."*
  - Camera capture abstraction allowing mockable image inputs for testability.
- `ai_meal_confirmation_card.dart` / `AiMealConfirmationSheet`:
  - **Human-in-the-Loop Barrier**: Never commits raw AI predictions automatically.
  - Overall confidence score indicator (`High 95%`, `Moderate 72%`, `Low 45%`) and uncertainty disclosure.
  - Itemized food candidate cards:
    - Food name with `Verified Catalog Item` badge.
    - Editable portion stepper and unit selector (katori, spoons, roti, grams).
    - Real-time macro summary recalculation as portions change.
    - Remove candidate item button / Add missing item button.
  - Hinglish Coaching Bento card with friendly tips.
  - Statutory non-diagnostic disclosure: *"Nutritional values are approximate AI estimations, not medical prescription."*
  - **Manual Fallback Button**: *"Not quite right? Search food catalog manually"* routing directly to Phase 6 verified food search.
  - **Confirm & Log Action**: Persists all confirmed items into `DriftMealLogRepository`, enqueues outbox sync mutations, and refreshes the home dashboard.

### 2. App Router Integration (`lib/app/router/app_router.dart`)
- Register `/nutrition/ai-text` and `/nutrition/ai-photo` in `AppRouter`.
- Wire direct launcher actions from `NutritionDashboardScreen` and `HomeDashboardScreen`.

### 3. Widget & Scenario Tests (`test/features/ai/ai_meal_logging_screen_test.dart`)
- Scenario 1: **Text Meal Analysis Flow**: Enters *"2 phulkas and dal"*, inspects candidate items, changes roti count from 2 to 3, confirms, and verifies row inserted into Drift.
- Scenario 2: **Photo Food Flow**: Analyzes mock meal photo, renders candidates with confidence badge, and commits to meal log.
- Scenario 3: **Uncertainty & Manual Fallback**: When confidence is low, uncertainty notice is shown; tapping "Search manually" navigates to catalog search.
- Scenario 4: **Statutory Guardrail Verification**: Asserts statutory non-medical disclaimer is prominently rendered on all confirmation screens.

<objective>
Implement Health Background Sync Manager, Battery Safeguards & OS Health UI (TASK 053, UI Integration).

Purpose: Protect mobile battery life with interval constraints and backoff retry logic, and equip users with clear transparency into OS health connection states, granted permissions, and manual sync triggers.
Output: Background sync manager, OS health sync card and settings screen, and automated tests.
</objective>

<threat_model>
### Security Threat Model (ASVS L1)
- **Threat**: Battery drain and background throttling triggering OS-level process termination.
- **Severity**: Low
- **Mitigation**: Strictly throttle sync frequency (minimum 15-minute intervals) and clamp window queries to incremental tokens.
</threat_model>

    Build HealthBackgroundSyncManager:

    - executeBackgroundSync(String userId):
      - Checks interval throttling
      - Invokes HealthIntegrationService.syncHealthData
      - Tracks consecutive failure count and calculates exponential backoff ($2^{attempts}$ min)
      - Logs failures to AppLogger with PII scrubbing
      - Returns BackgroundSyncResult (status: success/throttled/error, importedCount, message).

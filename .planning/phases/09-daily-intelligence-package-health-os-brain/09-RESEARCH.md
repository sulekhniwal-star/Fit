# Phase 9 Research: Daily Intelligence Package (DIP) & Health OS Brain

## 1. Domain Background & Health OS Synthesis
- **The Core Problem**: Most fitness apps present a bewildering grid of disconnected charts (steps, calories, macros, heart rate, sleep graphs). Users suffer cognitive overload and struggle to understand *what action to take today*.
- **The FitKarma Solution**: The Daily Intelligence Package (DIP) acts as an intelligent, deterministic synthesis layer. It acts like an expert health coach reviewing the user's data on app boot, computing:
  1. An interpretable **Daily Readiness Score** (0–100) reflecting physical recovery.
  2. A single **Primary Focus Signal** for today.
  3. Up to **Top 3 High-Impact Micro-Actions** tailored to time of day, recovery status, and logged habits.
- **Offline & Deterministic Guarantee**:
  - Operates purely offline in Dart using local encrypted Drift tables.
  - Generates zero unpredictable LLM hallucination risk or token cost for daily home boots.

## 2. Daily Readiness Score Mathematical Model
The Daily Readiness Score $R \in [0, 100]$ synthesizes 4 core physiological dimensions:

$$R = \frac{\sum_{i \in \text{Available}} w_i \cdot F_i}{\sum_{i \in \text{Available}} w_i}$$

Where the nominal weights and factors are:
1. **Sleep Factor ($F_{\text{sleep}}$, Weight $w_1 = 0.35$)**:
   - Combines total sleep duration relative to 8-hour baseline ($\text{score} = \text{clamp}(0, 100, (\text{duration\_minutes} / 480) \times 100)$) and subjective quality rating (1–5 scale scaled to 20–100).
   - If deep/REM stages are available from wearable, stage composition provides bonus fidelity.
2. **Resting Vitals Factor ($F_{\text{vitals}}$, Weight $w_2 = 0.25$)**:
   - Evaluates resting heart rate (RHR) against baseline. RHR in nominal zone (55–70 bpm) yields 90–100. Elevation of $> 5\text{ bpm}$ above personal 7-day moving average indicates autonomic stress/illness, reducing score.
3. **Workload & Strain Factor ($F_{\text{strain}}$, Weight $w_3 = 0.25$)**:
   - Evaluates Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR):
     - $\text{ACWR} \in [0.8, 1.3] \rightarrow 100$ (Optimal sweet spot)
     - $\text{ACWR} \in [1.3, 1.5] \rightarrow 70$ (Elevated fatigue)
     - $\text{ACWR} > 1.5 \rightarrow 40$ (High strain / injury risk)
     - $\text{ACWR} < 0.8 \rightarrow 90$ (Fresh / high readiness)
4. **Hydration & Nutritional Rhythm ($F_{\text{nutrition}}$, Weight $w_4 = 0.15$)**:
   - Compares consumed water and caloric distribution against target pace for the current hour of the day.

### Readiness Tiers & Explanations
- **85–100 (Peak Readiness)**: High recovery capacity. Ideal for progressive overload, heavy compound lifts, or strenuous cardio.
- **70–84 (Balanced / Steady)**: Stable recovery. Normal scheduled workout and daily step goals recommended.
- **50–69 (Moderate Strain)**: Residual fatigue present. Prioritize form, lighter accessory work, or active recovery.
- **0–49 (Recovery Priority)**: High physiological strain or acute sleep deficit. Recommend rest, stretching, hydration, and sleep hygiene.

*Statutory Guardrail*: Readiness is explicitly labeled as a non-diagnostic lifestyle wellness indicator under Indian medical software guidelines.

## 3. Deterministic Micro-Action Rules Engine
Micro-actions are generated by modular, deterministic rules evaluated against `DipContextInput`:

| Rule ID | Trigger Condition | Emitted Micro-Action | Priority Boost | Cultural / Health Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RULE_HYDRATION_DEFICIT` | Current hour $\ge 12$ and water $< 40\%$ of daily target | "Drink 500ml Water" | +35 | Prevents afternoon dehydration lethargy in warm climates. |
| `RULE_POST_MEAL_WALK` | Lunch/dinner logged $> 500\text{ kcal}$ within last 60 mins | "Take a 10-min *Shatapawali* Walk" | +40 | Reduces post-prandial glucose spike using traditional Indian post-meal strolling habit. |
| `RULE_SEDENTARY_BREAK` | Current hour $\ge 16$ and steps $< 3,000$ | "Take a 15-min Step Refresh" | +30 | Encourages breaking prolonged desk sitting before evening. |
| `RULE_HIGH_FATIGUE_REST` | Readiness score $< 50$ or ACWR $> 1.5$ | "Swap Heavy Training for Gentle Asana / Walk" | +50 | Protects against overtraining injury and central nervous fatigue. |
| `RULE_DINNER_LOG_REMINDER` | Current hour $\ge 20$ (8 PM) and no dinner logged | "Log Today's Dinner" | +25 | Timely nutrition logging before sleep. |
| `RULE_SLEEP_PREP` | Current hour $\ge 22$ (10 PM) | "Begin Sleep Wind-Down" | +20 | Pre-bed relaxation guidance for optimal sleep latency. |
| `RULE_COLD_START_ONBOARD` | Day 1 or no logs recorded today | "Log Morning Hydration & Check-in" | +45 | Encourages daily habit formation for new users. |

## 4. Architectural Integration & Routing
- `RouteNames.dashboard` (`/dashboard`) currently routes to `HealthDashboardScreen`.
- We will assemble a new, unified `HomeDashboardScreen` that hosts the DIP surface as the primary interface:
  - Header: Time-contextual Indian greeting ("Namaste / Good Morning", localized user name, streak, sync badge).
  - Section 1: **Daily Readiness Bento Card** (large score ring, tier chip, explanation, and factor chips).
  - Section 2: **Top Micro-Actions Feed** (max 3 actionable cards with action buttons and dismiss affordance).
  - Section 3: **Quick Logging Action Bar** (1-tap floating/docked shortcuts: Log Water, Log Meal, Quick Workout, Log Weight).
  - Section 4: **Summary Metrics Bento Row** (Steps, Active Calories, Sleep, Water with navigation pills to detailed screens).
